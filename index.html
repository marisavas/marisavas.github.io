I am a research scientist working on Responsible AI, AI governance and social computing.

At the Department of Computer Science at UFMG, Brazil, I lead FIAR, an evidence-based framework to govern and audit AI systems over time. We apply it to health AI projects at NIAR-Saúde, in partnership with Brazil's Ministry of Health.

In social computing, I study misinformation, hate speech, the underrepresentation of social and cultural groups, and how AI is discussed across languages and media.

Before UFMG, I spent seven years at IBM Research, where I worked on evaluating conversational agents, auditing ML models for bias and studying misinformation. That work led to 16 granted US patents.

Current work

  • FIAR. A governance maturity model with evidence-based monitoring across the AI lifecycle.
  • LEME. A RAG assistant on Brazilian and international regulations for health and Responsible AI.
  • AI in public discourse. How AI is framed across languages and media, starting from YouTube in Brazil and the United States.
