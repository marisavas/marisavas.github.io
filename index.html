Marisa Vasconcelos

Researcher in Responsible AI

I develop sociotechnical approaches to evaluate AI systems, examine their implications for people and communities, and support accountable institutional governance.

Explore my research About my trajectory

Central research question

How can AI systems be evaluated, developed, and governed responsibly when their behavior and consequences are shaped by technical choices, social inequalities, cultural representation, human relationships, and institutional practices?

Research agenda

Three interconnected dimensions

These dimensions provide an editorial structure for the site. They are not separate research silos, and individual projects may contribute to more than one of them.

01

Responsible AI and Institutional Governance

How institutions can translate Responsible AI principles into evidence, accountability, and sustained oversight across the AI lifecycle.

02

Sociotechnical Evaluation of AI Systems

How system behavior should be evaluated in relation to people, context, fairness, limitations, and real-world conditions of use.

03

Human, Social, and Epistemic Dimensions of AI

How AI systems represent people and communities, shape public narratives, and encounter limits in culturally and relationally sensitive contexts.

Interdisciplinary inquiry

From systems to institutions

A continuous research trajectory

My current work extends this trajectory toward Responsible AI and institutional governance, while preserving a long-standing interest in how computational systems operate within human, social, cultural, and organizational contexts.
